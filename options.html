ℹ️ About

Manga OCR Extension v1.0.0

Extract text from manga pages using Optical Character Recognition. Supports both English and Japanese text with local, privacy-first processing.

📖 Privacy Notice: By default, all OCR processing happens locally in your browser. No images are sent to external servers unless you explicitly enable remote mode.
